How to Compress Your Photo to 50 KB

  1. Click Launch Compressor to load ShrinkMyPhoto with the 50 KB preset preselected.
  2. Drag and drop your photo or click Choose photo (you can also paste directly with Ctrl+V).
  3. Review your target size (50 KB) and chosen format.
  4. Click Shrink photo. The browser searches for the ideal quality setting in under 150ms.
  5. Click Download photo or Copy photo to use your newly compressed image immediately.
🔒 Sovereign Privacy Guarantee: Unlike traditional image compression services, ShrinkMyPhoto operates entirely inside your device's browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your personal identification documents and photos are never sent across the internet.
