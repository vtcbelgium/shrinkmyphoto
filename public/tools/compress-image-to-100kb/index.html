How to Compress Your Photo to 100 KB

  1. Click Launch Compressor to load ShrinkMyPhoto with the 100 KB preset preselected.
  2. Drag and drop your photo or click Choose photo (you can also paste directly with Ctrl+V).
  3. Review your target size (100 KB) and chosen format.
  4. Click Shrink photo. The browser searches for the ideal quality setting in under 150ms.
  5. Click Download photo or Copy photo to use your newly compressed image immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image to exactly 100KB?

Upload or drag your image into ShrinkMyPhoto, set the target size to 100 KB, and click 'Shrink photo'. The browser automatically calculates the optimal resolution and JPEG compression ratio to stay strictly under 100KB.

Is it safe to compress sensitive ID or passport photos here?

Yes, absolutely. ShrinkMyPhoto is 100% sovereign and client-side. Your photos never leave your device, are never uploaded to any server, and no cookies or trackers exist.

Which file formats are supported?

You can compress JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You can also convert between formats (e.g. PNG to compressed JPEG) directly in your browser.
